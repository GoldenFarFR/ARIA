## Objective (B's words, preserved)

Make every result measurement conditional on real executability, before
producing one more number.

## Decision it improves

All of them. As long as a non-executable gain counts as a gain, every hypothesis
tested downstream -- H9 included -- is validated or rejected on noise. This is
upstream of alpha research, not beside it.

## B's attack on the x1972 (mandate §14: the more extraordinary, the harder the attack)

Entry 8.33e-05, reserve at entry $8,434, exit `scale_out_complete`. The price is
not absurd and the pool is not dry, so not trivially disqualifiable. But x1972
means exiting ~197,100% of a position from a pool holding $8,434. Economically
impossible: the exit would drain the pool long before. The `realistic_*` field
models slippage, evidently not market impact at that scale.

The case that settles it: a trade at x4 with **reserve_usd = $4**. Four dollars.
Not executable, booked as a winner.

Reserve distribution across the 579: under $1,000 = 186 (32.1%), $1k-10k = 197,
over $10k = 196. **A third of the sample sits on pools where no useful size fits.**

## B's conclusion as capital owner (preserved verbatim in substance)

ARIA today has no proof of edge. Not "a weak edge": the absence of a valid
measurement. Three cumulative defects, each sufficient to invalidate the number:
one day covered, the mean carried by 2 trades, a third of the population not
executable. The +346% is not a result, it is an artifact. B rejects it not for
being too good, but because without the top 5 it is +3.6% over a single day.

## Capability requested

A return bounded by market impact, computed for a reference size (B proposes
$100, open to discussion -- the order of magnitude of the real pilot, not a
theoretical figure), plus a per-position executability marker. Non-executable
positions are NOT deleted: they are kept and EXCLUDED from aggregates, with their
count published next to every figure.

B explicitly requests **no trading behaviour change** here, only the measurement
layer -- therefore no human gate needed. Causal note from B: the depth used must
be the one known at each decision instant, never the final reserve.

## Success criterion

The three figures (mean, without top 2, without top 5) recomputed on the
executable population only, with `n_executable / n_total` displayed. The x1972
and the $4-reserve trade must fall out mechanically, with no ad-hoc rule aimed at
them by name.

## Rejection criterion B set in advance

If `realistic_final_multiplier` already bounds market impact correctly, the
request falls -- but then it must be explained how a $4 pool produces an
executable x4, and the x1972 becomes a separate anomaly to investigate.

## Priority set by B

Before any new collection campaign, including the birth detector.
