A pool detected the instant it's created has, by definition, no 5-minute price history yet -- it cannot pass this filter at detection time regardless of how fast it's detected. The real latency bottleneck for THIS pocket is `get_trending_pools()`'s own 120s scan cadence, which applies equally to pools DexPaprika already has indexed, not the initial pool-creation event. Building the WS subscription (a new per-DEX event decoder, a pending-enrichment queue with retry/TTL against DexPaprika's indexing lag, new tests) would add real complexity for zero measurable latency gain on the m5-surge pipeline as it exists today. **Banked, not discarded**: worth revisiting if/when a future pocket enters on pool-creation itself rather than a price surge (e.g. a "day-zero entry" variant) -- see `docs/task-backlog.md` if that variant is ever scoped.
