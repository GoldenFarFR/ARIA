[CODE] Subject : WS-EXIT holder-concentration check moved to a PRE-TRADE gate -- the post-entry reject was measured live and protects nothing
Date : 2026.08.20 / Probleme : the post-entry reject ported to WS-EXIT earlier the same day was measured against real live entries and does NOT protect: of the first 4 real entries at top_holder>=92%, ZERO were closed by it (2 exited on `trailing_stop` after ~36s, 2 were still open past 33s). By the time RugCheck's async backfill lands, the position has already paid the entry fee and taken the loss -- the reject only ever closes an already-losing position. Operator call: block at the source, and if the concentration cannot be established in time, reject rather than enter.
Solution : `_holder_concentration_gate()` called inside `_track_candidate_pumpportal` immediately BEFORE the confirmation dict is returned -- a blocked candidate produces no row at all, so no entry fee and no loss. Fail-closed (`HOLDER_GATE_FAIL_CLOSED=True`): a timeout, a provider error, OR a report carrying no holder data all block the entry, since an unenriched answer is not evidence a token is clean. The real cost is stated rather than hidden -- a RugCheck outage stops this pocket entering ENTIRELY, which is the intended trade given the measured 0.8% winrate of the >=97% band. Outages are counted under their own `blocked_holder_gate_unavailable` stats key, deliberately split from `blocked_holder_concentration`, so a silent provider failure can never read as "the filter is working well" while the pocket is simply not trading. THE FEASIBILITY CLAIM THAT BLOCKED THIS FOR MONTHS WAS MEASURED AND FOUND WRONG: the old comment ("an entry gate would reintroduce the multi-minute RugCheck wait") assumed filtering the ~40 events/min of the raw PumpPortal feed; only candidates that already cleared the liquidity filter ever reach the gate -- 1.2/min on this pocket, 2.63/min across both pockets sharing rugcheck's throttle, against a 13/min budget (`_MIN_INTERVAL_S=4.5`), so 5x of headroom. Real measured RugCheck latency is 0.17s; the 4.5s seen per call is this dome's OWN throttle, not the provider. A free zero-latency proxy was tried FIRST and rejected on real data rather than on principle: `vTokensInBondingCurve` from the creation payload diverged from RugCheck on 4 of 14 live tokens and returned impossible values (105-107%, since the field counts VIRTUAL tokens, not a supply fraction). Side finding: 6 pre-existing tests in this file were making a REAL network call to RugCheck on a synthetic mint (silently 400-ing) -- they now inject the gate, closing a real "never a real network call" violation. 9 new tests, 264 targeted + coherence green. — `solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`, `test_solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`.

[CODE] Subject : pre-trade gate decisions persisted -- a blocked candidate left ZERO trace, so the filter could never be judged
Date : 2026.08.20 / Probleme : the pre-trade holder gate's entire value is that a blocked candidate never becomes a position -- no row in the pocket's table, no entry fee, no loss. That is also its measurement problem: the rejection left zero trace, so the top holder pct, the gate's real latency and the price we would have paid vanished the instant the coroutine returned. Nothing could answer the only question that matters -- "is the filter saving money, or cutting the winners?". Made concrete within minutes of going live: a candidate at top_holder=96.8% was observed entering with no `entry BLOCKED` line anywhere, and there was no way to tell whether the gate had been skipped, had seen a different (lower) pct at decision time, or had failed silently.
Solution : `pretrade_rejection_log.py` (`fresh_launch_pretrade_gate_log` table) records EVERY gate verdict at the moment it is made. `_holder_concentration_gate` now returns a `HolderGateOutcome` dataclass instead of a bare tuple, carrying `latency_ms` and `top_holder_pct` out on BOTH branches -- the accepted path included, since a filter can only be judged against what it let through and a table of rejections alone cannot tell a good cut from an indiscriminate one. Four deliberate honesty constraints, each covered by its own test: (1) the stored would-be entry is the SLIPPAGE-ADJUSTED price (same `_apply_price_impact_and_fee` a real entry pays) -- a raw mid price compared against a real later price would flatter the filter; (2) tracking is bounded to `TRACKING_WINDOW_MINUTES=180`, matching the pockets' own MAX_HOLD, so the filter is never credited with a collapse it would not have been exposed to; (3) a pool that goes dark is marked `unresolvable`, never deleted -- dropping those would bias the result toward survivors; (4) a provider error during tracking leaves the row open rather than writing a false verdict. `avoided_pnl_summary()` deliberately reports a NEGATIVE avoided PnL when the filter cuts winners -- the failure mode this table exists to make visible rather than assumable. Writes are best-effort and never raise into the trading loop (losing a log row is bad, blocking an entry because logging failed is worse), and the forward-tracking sweep is bounded by `max_rows` since this table grows by every decision, far faster than the position tables it shadows. 12 new tests, 256 targeted + coherence green. — `pretrade_rejection_log.py`, `solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`, `test_pretrade_rejection_log.py`, `test_solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`.

[CODE] Subject : "holder concentration" on a Solana fresh launch is really the POOL's own unsold share -- the filter measures traction, not concentration
Date : 2026.08.20 / Probleme : operator asked whether the pre-trade top-holder check could read an artificially low number at block 0, and whether the bonding-curve address should be excluded. Verified live against RugCheck's raw `/tokens/{mint}/report` on 6 real tokens: in 5 of 6, `topHolders[0].owner` IS the pool address itself, with the second real holder at 0.01-0.39% (one token: top1=99.28%/pool, top2=0.24%, 256 holders). Confirmed directly on the very candidate seen entering at 96.8% earlier -- its top holder's owner matched that row's own `pool_address` byte for byte. So `HOLDER_CONCENTRATION_REJECT_PCT` does not measure concentration among wallets at all: it measures how much supply is still UNSOLD in the curve, i.e. the inverse of traction. The founding statistic (">=97% -> 0.8% winrate") really said "tokens nobody bought do not go up" -- true and usable, but an ADOPTION signal, not the anti-rug guardrail the name implies. The operator's stated risk is also inverted: the pct starts near 100% and DECREASES as people buy, so the check sees an artificially HIGH number on the youngest tokens, never a low one.
Solution : both signals now measured side by side, NEITHER threshold changed on this reasoning alone (changing a live threshold on same-sample reasoning is exactly what this project's anti-overfitting doctrine forbids). `rugcheck.RugCheckReport` gained `top_holders` as (pct, owner) pairs (the existing scalar left untouched so every current caller keeps its behavior); `_holder_pct_excluding_pool()` resolves the largest NON-pool holder, returning None rather than a flattering 0.0 when it cannot be established; `pretrade_rejection_log` stores it in a new `top_holder_excluding_pool_pct` column, added via a hot idempotent `ALTER TABLE` behind a `PRAGMA table_info` guard so accumulation starts immediately on the already-live table. 24h of paired data will say which of the two signals actually carries the performance. Second real finding in the same pass: `run_forever_pumpportal`'s outcome counters accumulated in memory and were NEVER emitted anywhere -- so a RugCheck outage silently fail-closing every entry was indistinguishable from a quiet market, precisely the failure this pocket's fail-closed design makes possible. Now logged every `STATS_LOG_EVERY_N_EVENTS=200` events (~5 min at the measured live feed rate of 37.7 events/min). 9 new tests, 226 targeted + coherence green. — `services/rugcheck.py`, `solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`, `pretrade_rejection_log.py`, `test_solana_fresh_launch_ws_exit_shadow.py`.
